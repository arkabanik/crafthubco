Our vision

A historic place with a new purpose.

The Lion Brewery has been part of Rochester's story for generations. We're working to bring part of it back into active use as The Craft Hub: a welcoming space built around local drinks, creativity, practical skills and community connection.

We're developing the site now, working through the necessary permissions and building relationships with local producers, organisations and community groups.

It will be a place where people can share what they know, discover something new and help shape the next chapter of this historic site.

What's coming to the Lion

Five principles, one building.

  • Meet Community first

    A welcoming place for local people, visitors, makers and independent businesses to connect.

  • Make Local production

    Brewing, distilling, product development and hands-on creative activity rooted in craft and experimentation.

  • Learn Skills exchange

    Workshops, demonstrations, talks and opportunities for people to share practical knowledge and experience.

  • Share Shared spaces

    Community events, tastings, collaborations and physical space for ideas to gather and grow.

  • Return Regenerative

    Responsible production, reuse, local partnerships and lasting value for Medway.

Skill sharing

A space shaped by shared skills.

The Craft Hub will create opportunities for people to teach, demonstrate and exchange skills: brewing, fermentation and food, art, heritage, gardening, repair, making and other practical crafts.

You won't need to be an expert. The aim is an open, welcoming environment where experience is shared across generations and communities.
